About us

Blinc Blonde is a boutique salon on Beaufort Street, where Highgate meets Mt Lawley. We are a small team of colourists and stylists. Blonde is our speciality, from soft lived-in balayage and fine baby lights to full platinum, and we bring the same care to brunettes, cuts, straightening and styling. We work exclusively with Aveda, and every big change is planned around the condition of your hair. If your blonde can't be reached safely in one visit, we map it across appointments, so you leave brighter each time and your hair stays strong.
